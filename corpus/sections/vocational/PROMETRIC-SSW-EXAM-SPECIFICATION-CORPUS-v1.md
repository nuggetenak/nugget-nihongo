## 1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW: THE SSW EXAM LANDSCAPE IN JAPAN

The Specified Skilled Worker (特定技能 — *Tokutei Ginou*) evaluation system is administered through a multi-tiered regulatory framework established by the Ministry of Justice (MOJ), Immigration Services Agency of Japan (ISA), and line ministries (MHLW, METI, MLIT, MAFF). 

While **Prometric Japan Co., Ltd.** operates as the dominant testing delivery infrastructure for Computer-Based Testing (CBT) both within Japan and across 15+ overseas sending countries (including Indonesia, Philippines, Vietnam, Myanmar, Nepal, India, Mongolia, Uzbekistan, and Sri Lanka), the total SSW examination ecosystem is divided between:
1. **Prometric CBT-Administered Exams:** Covering general Japanese (JFT-Basic), Nursing Care (Skills & Specialized Japanese), Food Service (Tier 1 & 2), Food Manufacturing (Tier 1 & 2), Agriculture (Tier 1 & 2), Automobile Maintenance (Tier 1 & 2), Fisheries & Aquaculture (Tier 1 & 2), Construction (Tier 1 & 2), Accommodation (Tier 1 & 2), Industrial Product Manufacturing (Tier 1 & 2 across 17 divisions), Wood Industry (Tier 1), and the **Business Career Kentei for SSW** (Tier 2 prerequisite).
2. **Specialized Agency-Administered Exams:** Administered directly by national industry federations for sectors requiring heavy machinery yard performance or specialized physical simulation (Building Cleaning by JBMA, Shipbuilding by ClassNK, Aviation Ground Handling/MRO by JAAM/JAC, Automobile Transport by MLIT, Railway Operations by MLIT, and Forestry by JAFCO).

### 1.1 The Dual-Tier Status Hierarchy: 1-gō vs. 2-gō

| Attribute | Tokutei Ginō 1-gō (Tier 1) | Tokutei Ginō 2-gō (Tier 2) |
|---|---|---|
| **Period of Stay** | Capped at 5 years cumulative | Indefinite renewal (Pathway to Permanent Residency / *Eijū*) |
| **Family Accompaniment** | Prohibited (spouses/children cannot join) | Permitted (spouse and dependent children eligible for Dependent visa) |
| **Language Prerequisite** | CEFR A2 / JLPT N4 / JFT-Basic | Equivalent to JLPT N3–N2 + high-stakes supervisory register |
| **Workplace Role** | Frontline execution under supervision; task compliance | Line supervisor (*Shikisha*), team leader (*Shukuchō*), shift manager |
| **Eligible Sectors (2024–2026)** | All 18 designated sectors | 11 of 12 consolidated sectors (All except *Kaigo*, which has direct PR via *Kaigo Fukushishi*) |
| **Core Linguistic Demand** | Receptive procedural comprehension, safety shouting, basic HORENSO | Productive task delegation, conflict negotiation, incident reports, technical mentoring |

### 1.2 The Four Acquisition Pathways (*Shutoku Rūto*)

1. **Direct Prometric Examination Route (試験ルート):**
   - Candidate passes JFT-Basic (or JLPT N4+) and the sector-specific Technical Skill Assessment (TSA) CBT delivered via Prometric in Indonesia or Japan.
   - Primary target of Nugget Nihongo's A1 $\rightarrow$ A2/N4 foundational curriculum and Layer 2 SSW sub-decks.
2. **Technical Intern Transition Route (技能実習からの移行ルート):**
   - Candidates who successfully completed Technical Intern Training Tier 2 (*Ginō Jisshū 2-gō* — 3 years) are **fully exempt** from both the Japanese language test and the sector skills evaluation test if transferring into the same job cluster.
   - *Linguistic Pathology:* These learners possess fluent conversational *tameguchi* (plain form) and gemba jargon, but suffer severe deficits in written documentation, kanji recognition, and polite sociopragmatic registers (*keigo*).
3. **International Student Conversion Route (留学生からの変更ルート):**
   - Vocational college (*Senmon Gakkou*) or university graduates in Japan converting to SSW; require specialized industrial vocabulary bridge.
4. **The 2027 Statutory Pipeline: Ikusei Shūrō (育成就労制度):**
   - Enacted by the Japanese National Diet in 2024 to replace the Technical Intern system. Establishes a formal 3-year statutory training pipeline explicitly designed to graduate workers into Tokutei Ginō 1-gō (requiring A1/N5 entry and A2/N4 transition, with N3 benchmark for 2-gō).

## 3. ATOMIZED ANALYSIS OF COMPLEX SECTORAL DIVISIONS

### 3.1 Industrial Product Manufacturing: The 17 JAIM Operational Divisions (製造分野 17業務区分)

Following the regulatory integration administered by the Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry (METI) and the Japan Association for Human Resources in Industrial Product Manufacturing (JAIM), the industrial manufacturing sector is divided into **17 discrete operational divisions** (*gyōmu kubun*):

#### Division 1: 機械金属加工 (Machining & Metal Processing)
- **CBT Task Demands:** NC lathe programming, milling machine operation, sheet metal stamping, precision grinding, dimensional tolerance inspection.
- **Layer 2 Core Lexicon:**
  - 旋盤 (*semban* [0]) — Bubut / Lathe
  - フライス盤 (*furaisuban* [3]) — Mesin frais / Milling machine
  - 寸法公差 (*sumpō-kōsa* [4]) — Toleransi dimensi
  - 切削抵抗 (*sessaku-teikō* [4]) — Hambatan potong
  - バリ取り (*baritori* [0]) — Pembersihan geram / Deburring
- **Critical Safety Register:** 非常停止ボタン押せ！ (*Hijō-teishi botan ose!* — Tekan tombol emergency stop!); 切りくず触るな！ (*Kirikuzu sawaru na!* — Jangan sentuh tatal panas!).

#### Division 2: 電気電子機器組立て (Electric & Electronic Equipment Assembly)
- **CBT Task Demands:** PCB surface-mount technology (SMT), lead-free hand soldering, wiring harness routing, continuity and insulation resistance testing.
- **Layer 2 Core Lexicon:**
  - 基板実装 (*kiban-jissō* [4]) — Pemasangan komponen PCB
  - はんだ付け (*handadzuke* [0]) — Penyolderan
  - 静電気破壊 (*seidenki-hakai* [5]) — Kerusakan elektrostatis (ESD)
  - 短絡 (*tanraku* [0]) / ショート (*shōto* [1]) — Korsleting listrik
  - 導通検査 (*dōtsū-kensa* [4]) — Uji kontinuitas kabel
- **Critical Safety Register:** 通電中！触るな！ (*Tsūdenchū! Sawaru na!* — Arus menyala! Jangan sentuh!); アース確認しろ！ (*Āsu kakunin shiro!* — Periksa grounding!).

#### Division 3: 金属表面処理 (Metal Surface Treatment & Plating)
- **CBT Task Demands:** Electroplating tank maintenance, chemical degreasing, anodizing aluminum, acid pickling, effluent pH neutralization.
- **Layer 2 Core Lexicon:**
  - 電気めっき (*denki-mekki* [4]) — Elektroplating
  - 脱脂槽 (*dasshisō* [0]) — Bak degreasing pembersih minyak
  - 陽極酸化 (*yōkyoku-sanka* [4]) — Anodisasi
  - 酸洗い (*suarai* [0]) — Pencucian asam / Pickling
  - 廃水処理 (*haisui-shori* [4]) — Pengolahan air limbah
- **Critical Safety Register:** 強酸飛散注意！ (*Kyōsan hisan chūi!* — Awas percikan asam pekat!); 排気ファン回せ！ (*Haiki fan mawase!* — Nyalakan exhaust!).

#### Division 4: 紙器・段ボール箱製造 (Paper Container & Corrugated Board Manufacturing)
- **CBT Task Demands:** Corrugator machine operations, flexographic printing, die-cutting, folding-gluing, burst strength testing.
- **Layer 2 Core Lexicon:**
  - 段ボール原紙 (*danbōru-genshi* [4]) — Kertas medium karton
  - コルゲータ (*korugēta* [2]) — Mesin corrugator pembuat karton gelombang
  - 罫線 (*keisen* [0]) — Garis lipatan karton (creasing)
  - 糊付け (*noridzuki* [0]) — Pengeleman flap
  - 耐圧強度 (*taiatsu-kyōdo* [4]) — Kekuatan tekan box
- **Critical Safety Register:** ローラーに巻き込まれる！ (*Rōrā ni makikomareru!* — Jangan sampai terjepit roller!).

#### Division 5: コンクリート製品製造 (Precast Concrete Product Manufacturing)
- **CBT Task Demands:** Rebar cage assembly, steel formwork oiling, steam curing chamber monitoring, demolding, crack inspection.
- **Layer 2 Core Lexicon:**
  - プレキャスト型枠 (*purekyasuto-katawaku* [5]) — Cetakan beton pracetak
  - 蒸気養生 (*jōki-yōjō* [4]) — Curing uap beton
  - 脱型 (*dakkata* [0]) — Pembongkaran cetakan
  - 締め固め (*shimegatame* [4]) — Pemadatan beton (vibrator)
  - 配筋検査 (*haikin-kensa* [4]) — Inspeksi susunan besi tulangan
- **Critical Safety Register:** 型枠倒れるぞ！退避！ (*Katawaku taoreru zo! Taihi!* — Cetakan miring/roboh! Menjauh!).

#### Division 6: RPF製造 (Refuse Paper & Plastic Fuel Manufacturing)
- **CBT Task Demands:** Industrial waste sorting, primary/secondary shredder feeding, magnetic separation, pelletizing extruder operation.
- **Layer 2 Core Lexicon:**

#### Division 8: 印刷・製本 (Printing & Bookbinding)

- **Layer 2 Core Lexicon:**
  - オフセット枚葉機 (*ofusetto-maiyōki* [5]) — Mesin cetak lembaran offset
  - 網点 (*amiten* [0]) — Titik halftone cetak
  - 見当合わせ (*kentō-awase* [4]) — Registrasi presisi warna

#### Division 9: 紡織製品製造 (Textile / Spinning & Weaving Manufacturing)
- **CBT Task Demands:** Yarn spinning frame creeling, air-jet loom beam setting, cloth defect detection, tension gauge adjustment.
- **Layer 2 Core Lexicon:**
  - 紡績 (*bōseki* [0]) — Pemintalan benang

#### Division 10: 縫製 (Sewing & Apparel Manufacturing)

### 3.2 Construction: The 3 Consolidated JAC Classifications (建設分野 3大括り区分)

- **Division 1: 土木 (Civil Engineering):** Covers earthworks (*dokō*), road paving (*hosō*), bridge foundations (*kyōryō*), river dredging, and tunneling. Key term: 盛土 (*morido* [0] — Timbunan tanah), 掘削 (*kussaku* [0] — Penggalian).
- **Division 2: 建築 (Architecture & Building):** Covers formwork carpenting (*katawaku*), rebar tying (*tekkon*), plastering (*sakan*), interior drywall finishing (*naisō*), and scaffolding assembly (*ashiba*). Key term: 墨出し (*sumidashi* [0] — Penandaan garis as/elevasi), フルハーネス (*furu-hānesu* [3] — Full body harness).
- **Division 3: ライフライン・設備 (Lifelines & Utilities Equipment):** Covers water supply piping (*kyūsui-haikan*), duct installation (*dakuto*), indoor electrical wiring (*okunai-haisen*), and HVAC cooling systems. Key term: 水圧試験 (*suiatsu-shiken* [4] — Uji tekanan air), 漏電遮断器 (*rōden-shadanki* [5] — ELCB pemutus kebocoran listrik).

## 4. BUSINESS CAREER KENTEI FOR SSW TIER 2 (特定技能外国人向けビジネス・キャリア検定試験)

Administered by the **Japan Vocational Ability Development Association (JAVADA)** on Prometric CBT, passing this examination is the statutory prerequisite for obtaining **Tokutei Ginō 2-gō (Tier 2)** residence status in Manufacturing:

### 4.1 Exam Structure & Eligibility
- **Format:** Computer-Based Testing (CBT), 40 questions, 100-minute duration.
- **Passing Standard:** 60% correct answers (24 out of 40 questions).
- **Language:** Japanese ONLY (no bilingual glosses permitted during the live exam).
- **Target Fields:**
  1. **Level 3 Production Management Planning (生産管理プランニング 3級):** Product engineering, process design, master production schedule (MPS), bill of materials (BOM), plant layout, capacity planning.
  2. **Level 3 Production Management Operations (生産管理オペレーション 3級):** Daily scheduling, progress control, quality control (QC), inventory management, safety and 5S, equipment maintenance (TPM), procurement.

### 4.2 Core Curricular Syllabus for Business Career Kentei (Nugget Nihongo Module)

## 5. SECTORS ADMINISTERED BY NON-PROMETRIC INDUSTRY AGENCIES

For complete curriculum coverage across all 18 SSW sectors, Nugget Nihongo addresses the evaluation tests administered outside Prometric:

### 5.1 Building Cleaning (ビルクリーニング)
- **Exam Organization:** Japan Building Maintenance Association (全国ビルメンテナンス協会 — JBMA).
- **Test Format:** Practical performance test (flooring stripping, polishing, squeegee window cleaning) + Judgement written paper.
- **Key Terms:** 床面ワックス塗布 (*Yukamen wakkusu tofu* — Pelapisan lilin lantai), 希釈倍率 (*Kishaku bairitsu* — Rasio pengenceran kimia).

### 5.2 Shipbuilding & Ship Machinery (造船・舶用工業)
- **Exam Organization:** ClassNK (一般財団法人日本海事協会).
- **Sub-Trades (6 trades):** 溶接 (Welding), 塗装 (Painting), 鉄工 (Ironworking), 仕上げ (Finishing), 機械加工 (Machining), 電気機器組立て (Electrical Assembly).
- **Key Terms:** 船体ブロック搭載 (*Sentai burokku tōsai* — Pemasangan blok lambung kapal), スパッタ除去 (*Supatta jokyo* — Pembersihan percikan las).

### 5.3 Aviation: Ground Handling & Maintenance (航空業)
- **Exam Organization:** Japan Airport Ground Handling Association (JAGHA) / Aircraft Maintenance Association.
- **Sub-Trades:** グランドハンドリング (Ramp handling, ULD loading, pushback towing) / 航空機整備 (Aircraft line maintenance).
- **Key Terms:** プッシュバック (*Pusshubakku* — Pendorongan mundur pesawat), マーシャラー (*Māsharā* — Pemandu parkir pesawat).

### 5.4 Automobile Transport: Truck, Bus, Taxi (自動車運送業 — 2024 Cabinet Expansion)
- **Exam Organization:** Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism (MLIT).
- **Mandatory Hurdle:** Must pass the Japanese Class 2 Professional Driver's License examination (第二種運転免許学科試験) in Japanese + Customer service / Emergency evacuation test.
- **Key Terms:** 飲酒検知器 (*Inshu-kenchiki* — Alat deteksi alkohol napas), 運行前点呼 (*Unkōmae tenko* — Apel pemeriksaan kesiapan jalan), 荷締め機 (*Nijimeki* — Tali pengikat muatan / Ratchet tie-down).

### 5.5 Railway Operations & Maintenance (鉄道業 — 2024 Cabinet Expansion)
- **Exam Organization:** Designated Railway Safety Organization / MLIT.
- **5 Sub-Categories:** 軌道整備 (Track maintenance), 電気設備 (Catenary wire/signals), 車両整備 (Bogie/brake maintenance), 車両製造 (Carriage assembly), 運輸係員 (Station staff/train conductors).
- **Key Terms:** 建築限界 (*Kenchiku genkai* — Batas ruang bebas jalur rel), 指差確認 (*Shisa kakunin* — Tunjuk-sebut keselamatan).

### 5.6 Forestry (林業 — 2024 Cabinet Expansion)
- **Exam Organization:** Japan Forestry Association (全国林業改良普及協会 — JAFCO).
- **Test Scope:** Chainsaw safety certificate (伐倒特別教育), felling direction calculation, cable crane rigging, steep slope footing.
- **Key Terms:** つる (*Tsuru* [0] — Engsel kayu penahan arah jatuh), 退避路確保 (*Taihiro kakuho* — Pengamanan jalur evakuasi sebelum menebang).
