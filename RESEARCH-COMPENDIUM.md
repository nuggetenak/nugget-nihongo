## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & REPOSITORY PURPOSE

This repository contains the exhaustive Second Language Acquisition (SLA), psycholinguistic, and cognitive science research compendium underpinning the original curriculum of **Nugget Nihongo**. Designed specifically for Indonesian L1 speakers acquiring Japanese, the platform synthesizes over 750 peer-reviewed studies across 16 core thematic domains, level-ladder specifications, and algorithmic design matrices.

All findings are operationalized into reproducible curriculum parameters:
1. **Contrastive Linguistics & Error Analysis:** Direct empirical mapping of Indonesian ↔ Japanese grammatical, lexical, and phonological interference points.
2. **Cognitive Load & Memory Optimization:** Free Spaced Repetition Scheduling (FSRS) calibrated with Indonesian-specific difficulty priors.
3. **High-Variability Phonetic Training (HVPT):** Multi-speaker acoustic modeling to address mora-timing and phonemic discrimination challenges.
4. **Pragmatic & Sociolinguistic Calibration:** Explicit honorific (*keigo*) and speech-act instructional ladders mitigating workplace anxiety (*malu*).

### 3.2 Resolution of Major Literature Gaps

#### 1. Resolution of EA-22 & Expansion of Indonesian L1 Empirical SLA Evidence
The previous literature gap marker `EA-22` has been replaced with rigorous, peer-reviewed empirical studies directly examining Indonesian L1 learners acquiring Japanese:
- **`[EA-22]` Maarif, S. (2023).** *The acquisition of Japanese case particles by Indonesian learners of Japanese: Focusing on the concept of kou "argument" of verb.* *JAPANEDU*, 8(2), 127–138. DOI: `10.17509/japanedu.v8i2.62701`. Proves that case particle substitution (*ni* for *de*) and omission stem directly from Indonesian prepositional equivalence assumptions and mismatched verbal argument structures.
- **`[EA-23]` Prihantoro et al. (2025).** *DICO-JALF v.1.0: Diponegoro Corpus of Japanese Learners as a Foreign Language in Indonesia.* *Jurnal Arbitrer*, 12(3), 274–288. DOI: `10.25077/ar.12.3.274-288.2025`. Provides large-scale learner corpus distributions of morphosyntactic and lexical errors.
- **`[EA-24]` Haristiani, N., & Christinawati, D. (2024).** *Interlanguage pragmatic competence of university students: An error analysis of apology speech act strategies.* *IJOLE*, 8(1), 162–177. DOI: `10.26858/ijole.v8i1.60904`. Documents negative pragmatic transfer in apology and politeness routines.
- **`[EA-25]` Husna, L., & Febriyanti, R. (2022).** *Japanese native speakers' perception on learners' pronunciation of double consonant sounds (sokuon).* *JAPANEDU*, 7(1), 58–69. DOI: `10.17509/japanedu.v7i1.44578`. Quantifies native acoustic perception thresholds for mora-timing and geminate closure shortfalls.
- **`[EA-26]` Prihantoro et al. (2024).** *A corpus-based syntactic error analysis of Japanese learners' writing in LPK Cahaya Mandiri Indonesia.* *JRLLC*, 6(2), 118–135. DOI: `10.33633/jr.v6i2.11252`. Corroborates vocational trainee error distributions in high-stakes Specified Skilled Worker (SSW) preparation.

#### 2. Canonical Assignment of Matsunaga (1999) `[OD-20]`
Formally assigned canonical citation ID **`[OD-20]`**:
- **Matsunaga, S. (1999).** *The role of kanji knowledge transfer in acquisition of Japanese as a foreign language.* *Japanese Language and Literature*, 33(1), 1–23. DOI: `10.20649/00000278`.
- **Pedagogical Significance:** Provides the foundational empirical baseline establishing that learners without kanji background require **2.3× more exposures** per character to attain recognition parity with Chinese/Korean-L1 learners. This empirically dictates the calibrated difficulty priors for kanji cards within Nugget Nihongo's FSRS scheduling algorithm.

### 3.3 Reconciliation of Design Decision Master (DDM) Cross-Conflicts

In `blueprint/DESIGN-DECISION-MASTER-v1.md`, all cross-section theoretical tensions were resolved into unambiguous engineering and pedagogical directives:

1. **Flag A — Audio Quality Standards (HVPT vs. Synthetic TTS):**
   - *Resolution:* Multi-speaker natural human-recorded audio (minimum 2 native speakers per lexical item: 1 male, 1 female) is the **normative gold standard** dictated by High-Variability Phonetic Training research (`PH-30`–`PH-39`) and ego-permeability internalization (`AL-17`, `AL-18`). Perceptual learning fails to generalize across acoustic environments with synthetic voices alone. High-quality neural TTS is permitted strictly as an interim development scaffold and must be systematically replaced prior to curriculum deployment.
2. **Flag B — Notification Timing & Sleep Consolidation:**
   - *Resolution:* The platform's default notification window is anchored at **19:00–22:00**, directly grounded in hippocampal memory replay and slow-wave sleep (SWS) consolidation evidence (`SL-03`, `SL-04`). Pre-sleep study proximity significantly enhances 6-month retention. Concurrently, respecting Self-Determination Theory (SDT) autonomy support (`AL-01`–`AL-04`), learners maintain full configuration autonomy to customize notification schedules in platform settings.
3. **Flag C — Multimedia Enrichment vs. Seductive Details:**
   - *Resolution:* Grounded in Cognitive Load Theory (`CL-01`–`CL-08`, `CT-01`–`CT-18`) and empirical findings on seductive details in MALL (`CM-18`), decorative or gamified visuals that are incidental to learning content impair schema formation. All visual assets must be **content-intrinsic and schema-congruent**, depicting the semantic, situational, or cultural case frame of the lexical target.
