# SSW Dual-Mandate Curriculum Standard Rule

When authoring, auditing, or refactoring Specified Skilled Worker (SSW / 特定技能) curriculum tracks, vocabulary, dialogues, and drills across Nugget Nihongo:

1. **Dual-Mandate Equivalence**:
   - Every SSW track must strictly reflect the official Japanese sector ministry textbooks (MLIT, MAFF, MHLW, METI) and fulfill two simultaneous objectives:
     (a) **Prometric Certification Test Parity**: Align directly with the published Prometric evaluation test catalog, question types, and technical terminology (cross-referenced with `PROMETRIC_CATALOG_CROSSWALK.json`).
     (b) **Gemba Workplace Operational Readiness**: Enforce authentic Japanese workplace operational standards, including **5S** (Seiri, Seiton, Seisou, Seiketsu, Shitsuke), **KYT** (Kiken Yochi Training / Hazard Prediction), **Ho-Ren-So** (Houkoku, Renraku, Soudan), and sector-specific occupational safety regulations.

2. **Sector-Specific Supervisor Personas & Dialogues**:
   - NEVER use generic factory manager (`Koujoucho`) boilerplate in non-manufacturing sectors.
   - Hotel/Accommodation must feature the Head Concierge / Front Office Manager; Food Service must feature the Head Chef / Floor Manager; Fishery must feature the Boat Captain / Crew Chief; Aviation must feature the Flight Line Chief / Ground Handling Supervisor.

3. **Zero Orphaned Sector Terminology**:
   - All 36 curated industry vocabulary terms per sector must be actively integrated into lesson dialogues, safety manuals, and micro-drills across the syllabus, leaving zero orphaned terms in glossary files.
