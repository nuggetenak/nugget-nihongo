# Objective Critical Thinking, Anti-Fragile Brainstorming & Debt Ceiling Rule

When planning, evaluating, or refactoring curricula, features, and architectures across Nugget Nihongo:

1. **Zero Sycophancy & Mandatory Red-Teaming**:
   - Never passively validate unbounded scope inflation, hypothetical roadmaps (e.g. 1000 passes), or resume-driven technology piling that lacks empirical utility.
   - For every major architectural proposal, conduct an explicit **Red-Team / Devil's Advocate critique** exposing the hidden maintenance tax, regression risks, and bundle penalties.
   - Ground all architectural evaluations in real codebase metrics: bundle sizes, parse times on budget mobile devices (INP), and measurable pedagogical depth.

2. **The Pre-Mortem & Boring Technology Heuristics**:
   - Before committing to multi-week plans, execute a **Pre-Mortem Inversion**: Assume the next major institutional showcase fails catastrophically before lecturers and LPK directors. Identify the top 3 failure causes (e.g., bad furigana, frozen UI on mobile, rushed dummy content) and make preventing them the immediate top priority.
   - Follow Dan McKinley's **Choose Boring Technology** principle: Never introduce a new backend server, ORM, or router unless the existing stack (React 18 + Vite + IndexedDB + Supabase REST) physically cannot fulfill the requirement.

3. **Technical Debt Ceiling & Mandatory Feature Freeze**:
   - When open `CRITICAL` and `HIGH` defects in `FINDINGS_LEDGER.csv` exceed 20, enforce a strict **GLOBAL FEATURE FREEZE**.
   - Zero new frameworks, zero new packages in `package.json`, and zero new feature tracks until the debt in the active level (starting with Level N5) is completely remediated and verified.
   - Essential refactoring strictly required to fix critical bugs (e.g. infinite loops, memory leaks) is permitted and required.

4. **Single-Track Benchmark Principle (Standar Emas Sebelum Duplikasi)**:
   - Always perfect one core track (Jalur Mandiri N5) to 100% completion (zero dummy fallback generators, authentic situational dialogues, 5-tier micro-drills, thematic kanji, and Tri-Layer Acceptance verification) before scaling horizontally to other levels or tracks.
   - External textbooks (Minna no Nihongo, Genki, Try!) are structured as secondary companion tracks for classroom learners, ensuring the sovereign flagship curriculum remains clear, unfragmented, and friction-free for independent learners.

5. **Empirical Human Dogfooding Gate & Additive Safety**:
   - Synthetic green tests (`node tests/...`) are necessary but insufficient. No level or milestone is certified until verified through a hands-on **Checklist Dogfooding 10 Poin** on physical mobile viewports.
   - Maintain 100% green status across all historical cumulative regression test suites (`test-sprint-6-books.js`, `test-sprint-29-textbook-drills.js`, `test-sprint-27-jft-seikatsu.js`). Never disable or delete an existing test contract to force a new architectural slice to pass.

6. **The 4-Pillar Product Identity Invariant**:
   - Nugget Nihongo consists of 4 co-equal pillars:
     1. JLPT Core (N5–N1)
     2. JFT-Basic (A1 & A2)
     3. SSW Comprehensive (19 Tokutei Ginou Sectors & Prometric CBT)
     4. Living in Japan (Seikatsu Nihongo: Shiyakusho, Gomi, Sumai, Bousai)
   - Never regress or ignore Pillars 2, 3, and 4 in favor of academic JLPT drills alone.
   - Any roadmap or milestone proposal must explicitly declare which pillars are addressed and provide clear scheduling rationale for those deferred.

7. **AI Cognitive Failure Mode Self-Audit**:
   - Before presenting any plan or analysis, verify compliance against the 6 failure modes in the `deep-first-principles-brainstorming` skill (Resume-Driven Dev, Toxic Sycophancy, Green Test Mirage, Audit-Fix Amnesia, Scope Hallucination, Analysis Paralysis).

8. **Analysis Paralysis & Finite Discovery Gate**:
   - Planning and `/grill-me` interviews are capped at a maximum of 3 conversational iterations per initiative.
   - Once key constraints are surfaced, the agent MUST converge immediately to an empirical, executable Step 0 and Step 1 rather than opening further theoretical inquiries.

9. **Pain-Point Self-Introspection & Adaptive Immunization**:
   - The agent must continuously audit its own operational friction (e.g. tool retries, context amnesia, repetitive questioning, user corrections).
   - SEVERE friction (data destruction, security, build breakage): Immediate `/learn` on 1st occurrence.
   - MODERATE friction (context drift, state desync, factual corrections): Adapt immediately; `/learn` on 2nd occurrence.
   - MINOR friction (formatting, verbosity, naming): Note; `/learn` on 2nd occurrence.
